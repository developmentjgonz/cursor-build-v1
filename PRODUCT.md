# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Dilo serves mobile-first users who want to take financial actions on Solana without learning wallet, DEX, routing, slippage, or prediction-market interfaces. They use a connected wallet to describe an outcome in English or Spanish, review the exact transaction, and sign it themselves.

## Product Purpose

Dilo turns natural-language requests into validated Solana actions. Success means users can connect Phantom, describe a swap or prediction position, understand a clear Intent Receipt, explicitly approve it, sign locally, and receive confirmation without Dilo controlling their keys.

## Brand Personality

Calm, trustworthy, and direct. Dilo should combine Phantom's approachable simplicity with Cash App's clarity, using plain language and confident transaction feedback.

## Anti-references

Avoid dense trading terminals, neon crypto aesthetics, speculative casino cues, decorative financial charts, and interfaces that hide fees or conditions. The product should not resemble a generic AI chatbot or an exchange dashboard.

## Design Principles

- Lead with the user's intended outcome, not protocol terminology.
- Make every financial consequence visible before approval.
- Keep one primary action obvious at each step.
- Use progressive disclosure so the main flow stays calm and focused.
- Treat English and Spanish as equal first-class experiences.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus states, screen readers, reduced motion, color-safe semantic states, sufficient touch targets, and readable contrast. Never communicate transaction status through color alone.
